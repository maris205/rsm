# Figure caption

**Autonomous scalar background and drift-normalized electromagnetic response.** The canonical scalar with $V=V_s e^{-2\chi}$ is evolved jointly with a dust + cosmological-constant Friedmann background for illustrative ε = $10^{-6},10^{-4},10^{-2}$. Top left: departure of the dynamical field from the exact-log-time ansatz. Top right: scalar density fraction divided by ε. Bottom left: the ratio of the dynamical transfer to the exact-log transfer, evaluated on the same numerical cosmic-time history. Bottom right: the corresponding transfer functions; the dashed curve uses the middle-ε time history. The near overlap of the solid curves indicates the small effect of scalar backreaction over this illustrative range. Radiation and the homogeneous electromagnetic source are omitted. No data are fitted, and neither the ε values nor the response amplitude are claimed to satisfy observational bounds. PNG and PDF are generated by [run_action_background.py](../code/run_action_background.py).
